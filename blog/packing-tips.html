18 Packing Tips From Professional Movers

The small habits our crews see working — and the mistakes they rescue people from.

  1. Home
  2. Blog
  3. Packing Tips

The 18 Tips

  1. Never pack a heavy item in a big box. Books and plates go in small boxes — big ones are for light, bulky things like bedding.
  2. Keep boxes under 20kg. That's the safe carrying weight for a mover. Heavy boxes slow everyone down and get damaged.
  3. Tape the bottom of every box with a "H" of tape — two strips across and one along. Dozens of boxes fail at the bottom fold.
  4. Use wadding and paper, not just bubble wrap. Newspaper ink transfers; acid-free paper protects china and glass better.
  5. Plates travel on edge, like records — not flat. Stack them upright, separated by paper.
  6. Fill every gap. A box that moves inside a box is how things break. Use tea towels and clothes as free void-fillers.
  7. Label by room, not by list. "Kitchen — one of three" tells the crew exactly where to put it.
  8. Label the side, not the top. Boxes are carried from the sides — labels on top get covered by the box above.
  9. Keep an essentials box for each overnight. Kettle, mugs, teabags, loo roll, chargers, meds, a change of clothes.
  10. Pack the bed last and unpack it first. You'll thank yourself at 10pm.
  11. Use wardrobe boxes for hanging clothes. It saves the ironing marathon after a big move.
  12. Take drawers out for a heavy chest — or remove the contents and tape the drawers shut. The piece becomes half the weight.
  13. Wrap mirrors and artwork in blankets face-out and tape the corners — never the glass surface.
  14. Put screws and fittings in a labelled bag taped to the item they came from. No mystery hardware at the new house.
  15. Photograph electronics wiring before unplugging. Reassembling the TV cabinet becomes a five-minute job.
  16. Pack a small "open first" box with tools, light bulbs, and the kettle — for the new address.
  17. Don't pack the kettle for the last morning — it's the single most-repacked item in the country.
  18. Consider professional packing. It costs less than you think and takes the whole job off your plate — see our packing service.
