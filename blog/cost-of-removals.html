How Much Do Removals Cost in the UK in 2026?

A straight answer, with real numbers — so you know roughly what to expect before you get a quote.

  1. Home
  2. Blog
  3. Cost of Removals

The Short Answer

Most local house moves in the UK cost between £300 and £1,200, depending on size, distance and time of year. A man-and-van job might be £80–£300, while a long-distance move can run £500–£2,500+.

Those ranges are deliberately wide, because price is driven by specifics. Here's what actually moves the number.

Typical Prices by Property Size

Studio / 1-bed flat£250–£500

4-bed house£600–£1,300

Prices vary by region and season. These are typical UK ranges for 2026, not a quote.

What Drives the Cost

1. Property size and volume

More belongings mean a bigger vehicle and more crew hours. A 3-bed house typically needs a 3.5-tonne luton van and two movers for a full day — that's the core of the cost.

2. Distance

Longer runs cost more for fuel, driver time and travel. The jump from "local" to "cross-country" is often the biggest single price leap.

3. Access and stairs

Ground-floor with a drive? Cheap and easy. A second-floor flat with narrow stairs and no parking? The crew needs more time and skill. Expect a modest uplift.

4. Extra services

Full packing, dismantling/reassembly of furniture, and storage each add to the price — but they often save you money overall in time and risk.

5. Timing

Weekends, month-ends and school holidays are the busiest — and priciest. A midweek move can save you real money.

Fixed Quotes vs Hourly Rates

Hourly rates sound transparent but punish you for the unavoidable — traffic, a slow lift, a last-minute rethink. A fixed quote locks the price before you book, based on a proper inventory, and that's how we work.

Get a fixed price from us via the quote page — it's free, no obligation, and confirmed in writing.

How to Save Money on a Move

  • Book midweek, not a Friday or month-end.
  • Declutter before you get quoted — you pay to move things.
  • Pack boxes yourself if you have time (see our packing tips).
  • Get quotes from at least two reputable firms, not the cheapest anonymous van.

One Thing to Always Ask For

Goods-in-transit insurance. Professional firms carry £10m+ cover with clear terms. If a quote looks too good, ask what happens if something breaks.
