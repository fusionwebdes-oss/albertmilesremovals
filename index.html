FAQs

Removal Questions, Answered

How much does a house removal cost?

Every move is different, but most local West Midlands house removals range from £350 to £1,200 depending on the size of your home, the distance and the time of month. We provide a free, fixed-price quote with no obligation.

How far in advance should I book?

We recommend booking 2–4 weeks ahead, especially for weekends and month-ends. For urgent moves, call us — we can often fit smaller moves in within days.

Are you insured?

Yes. We carry up to £10m goods-in-transit insurance and public liability cover as standard, so your belongings are fully protected during the move.

Do you provide packing materials and packing services?

Absolutely. We supply boxes, tape, bubble wrap and blankets, and our team can pack your whole home for you — or just the fragile stuff. See our packing services for details.

Can you store our belongings?

Yes. We offer secure, monitored storage on flexible terms — ideal when your completion dates don't line up. See our storage page for more.
