      <h2>Pianos Are Not Sofas</h2>
      <p>A piano is heavy, fragile and irreplaceable. Move it wrong and you risk cracked soundboards, damaged strings and a tuning bill that dwarfs your moving costs. That's why our piano removals are carried out by trained, experienced handlers with the right equipment — never as an afterthought on a general move.</p>
      <p>We move upright pianos, digital pianos and grands across the West Midlands and nationwide. For grands we use purpose-built skids and boards, and we assess every route before moving a note. We'll take the legs and pedals off a grand safely, protect the keyboard and soundboard, and position the piano exactly where you want it in its new home.</p>
      <p>After every piano move we recommend a tuning appointment — no removal company can guarantee your piano will stay perfectly in tune through a journey, but a proper move keeps it as close as possible and avoids lasting damage.</p>
