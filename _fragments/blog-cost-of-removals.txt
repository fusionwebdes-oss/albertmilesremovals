      <h2>The Short Answer</h2>
      <p>Most local house moves in the UK cost between <strong>£300 and £1,200</strong>, depending on size, distance and time of year. A man-and-van job might be <strong>£80–£300</strong>, while a long-distance move can run <strong>£500–£2,500+</strong>. Looking for cheap house removals? A fixed-price quote is the best way to compare house removal costs fairly.</p>
      <p>Those ranges are deliberately wide, because price is driven by specifics. Here's what actually moves the number.</p>

      <h2>Typical Prices by Property Size</h2>
      <table>
        <thead><tr><th>Move type</th><th>Typical range</th></tr></thead>
        <tbody>
          <tr><td>Studio / 1-bed flat</td><td>£250–£500</td></tr>
          <tr><td>2-bed flat / house</td><td>£300–£650</td></tr>
          <tr><td>3-bed house</td><td>£400–£900</td></tr>
          <tr><td>4-bed house</td><td>£600–£1,300</td></tr>
          <tr><td>5+ bed house</td><td>£900–£2,000</td></tr>
          <tr><td>Man & van (local)</td><td>£80–£300</td></tr>
        </tbody>
      </table>
      <p class="muted">Prices vary by region and season. These are typical UK ranges for 2026, not a quote.</p>

      <h2>What Drives the Cost</h2>
      <h3>1. Property size and volume</h3>
      <p>More belongings mean a bigger vehicle and more crew hours. A 3-bed house typically needs a 3.5-tonne luton van and two movers for a full day — that's the core of the cost.</p>
      <h3>2. Distance</h3>
      <p>Longer runs cost more for fuel, driver time and travel. The jump from "local" to "cross-country" is often the biggest single price leap.</p>
      <h3>3. Access and stairs</h3>
      <p>Ground-floor with a drive? Cheap and easy. A second-floor flat with narrow stairs and no parking? The crew needs more time and skill. Expect a modest uplift.</p>
      <h3>4. Extra services</h3>
      <p>Full packing, dismantling/reassembly of furniture, and storage each add to the price — but they often save you money overall in time and risk.</p>
      <h3>5. Timing</h3>
      <p>Weekends, month-ends and school holidays are the busiest — and priciest. A midweek move can save you real money.</p>

      <h2>Fixed Quotes vs Hourly Rates</h2>
      <p>Hourly rates sound transparent but punish you for the unavoidable — traffic, a slow lift, a last-minute rethink. A <strong>fixed quote</strong> locks the price before you book, based on a proper inventory, and that's how we work.</p>
      <p>Get a fixed price from us via the <a href="../quote.html">quote page</a> — it's free, no obligation, and confirmed in writing.</p>

      <h2>How to Save Money on a Move</h2>
      <ul>
        <li>Book midweek, not a Friday or month-end.</li>
        <li>Declutter before you get quoted — you pay to move things.</li>
        <li>Pack boxes yourself if you have time (see our <a href="packing-tips.html">packing tips</a>).</li>
        <li>Get quotes from at least two reputable firms, not the cheapest anonymous van.</li>
      </ul>

      <h2>One Thing to Always Ask For</h2>
      <p>Goods-in-transit insurance. Professional firms carry £10m+ cover with clear terms. If a quote looks too good, ask what happens if something breaks. Remember, removal company prices vary widely — always compare fixed quotes in writing, not hourly estimates.</p>
