      <h2>The Three Most Common Failures</h2>
      <h3>1. No single owner</h3>
      <p>If everyone's responsible, no one is. Appoint one person as move manager with authority to make decisions — and give them a deadline.</p>
      <h3>2. IT last</h3>
      <p>IT is planned after furniture, then staff can't work for three days. Plan the server room and cabling first — everything else fits around it.</p>
      <h3>3. Desk-by-desk chaos</h3>
      <p>Without labelling and a floor plan for the new office, boxes land anywhere and the first morning is a treasure hunt.</p>

      <h2>The Blueprint</h2>
      <h3>8 weeks out</h3>
      <ul>
        <li>Set the move date and budget. Confirm who owns it.</li>
        <li>Tour the new space and map departments to rooms and desks.</li>
        <li>Get quotes from at least two <a href="../services/office-removals.html">office removal</a> firms.</li>
      </ul>
      <h3>4 weeks out</h3>
      <ul>
        <li>Book IT migration and telecoms for the new building.</li>
        <li>Label every desk, chair and screen with its new location.</li>
        <li>Identify anything that needs special handling — safes, servers, fragile displays.</li>
      </ul>
      <h3>1 week out</h3>
      <ul>
        <li>Communicate the plan to staff: what to pack, what to carry, what's not allowed.</li>
        <li>Test lifts, parking and access at the new address.</li>
        <li>Confirm insurance and any landlord rules on lift usage.</li>
      </ul>
      <h3>Move day</h3>
      <ul>
        <li>The crew works to your floor plan — rooms get packed and placed by department.</li>
        <li>A nominated person is present at both ends with authority and a phone.</li>
        <li>IT is installed first so desks can connect the moment they land.</li>
      </ul>

      <h2>The 24-Hour Rule</h2>
      <p>Aim for the business to be <strong>operational within 24 hours</strong>. That's the difference between a professional relocation and a logistical event. It's achievable if the plan above is followed.</p>
