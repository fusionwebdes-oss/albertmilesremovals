      <h2>The Three-Phase Move</h2>
      <p>Every move, however big, is three phases: <strong>prepare</strong>, <strong>move</strong>, <strong>settle</strong>. Nail each and the whole thing stays calm.</p>

      <h2>Phase 1: Prepare</h2>
      <ul>
        <li>Book your <a href="../services/house-removals.html">removal team</a> at least three weeks ahead for a weekend.</li>
        <li>Declutter — sell or donate what you don't use. You move less and pay less.</li>
        <li>Start packing the least-used rooms first (see the <a href="moving-house-checklist.html">full checklist</a>).</li>
        <li>Take a meter reading at your current home and tell your provider.</li>
      </ul>

      <h2>Phase 2: Move Day</h2>
      <ul>
        <li>Have a labelled essentials box ready — kettle, chargers, meds, a change of clothes.</li>
        <li>Be present to walk the crew through heavy items and fragile bits.</li>
        <li>Ask the crew to put boxes in the right rooms — it saves you hours.</li>
        <li>Leave valuables and paperwork with you, not the van.</li>
      </ul>

      <h2>Phase 3: Settle</h2>
      <ul>
        <li>Unpack the kitchen and bedrooms first — everything else can wait.</li>
        <li>Read both meters and photograph them.</li>
        <li>Set up the boiler, wifi and security before you collapse.</li>
        <li>Redirect your post for 12 months.</li>
      </ul>

      <h2>What Most People Get Wrong</h2>
      <p>Packing too late. The single biggest source of stress on move day is a family still packing while the crew waits. Aim to be fully packed by the night before — the crew arrives to a done job, and the day becomes almost pleasant.</p>
