      <h2>Why Long-Distance Is Different</h2>
      <p>A long-distance move isn't just a local move with a longer drive. The crew is away for a full day or more, fuel and driver time multiply, and the logistics — two properties in different towns, no room for a "quick dash back" — need a plan.</p>
      <p>That's why our <a href="../services/long-distance-removals.html">long-distance removal service</a> is handled as a dedicated process, with a confirmed fixed price that covers the whole job.</p>

      <h2>What Affects a Long-Distance Price</h2>
      <ul>
        <li><strong>Mileage</strong> — the distance between the two addresses, including return.</li>
        <li><strong>Vehicle size</strong> — matching the van to your volume so you're not paying for empty space.</li>
        <li><strong>Crew size</strong> — two or three movers depending on the property.</li>
        <li><strong>Timing</strong> — overnight stops only apply for Scotland and far-flung corners.</li>
        <li><strong>Insurance</strong> — full transit cover for the whole journey.</li>
      </ul>

      <h2>What to Ask Before You Book</h2>
      <ul>
        <li>Is the price fixed or an estimate? (Ours is fixed, in writing.)</li>
        <li>What's covered by insurance, and what's the excess?</li>
        <li>Who handles the vehicle if there's a breakdown en route?</li>
        <li>Can the crew call ahead with an ETA for your new home?</li>
      </ul>

      <h2>Prepare Your New Home</h2>
      <p>Long-distance moves arrive in the evening more often than local ones. Have the new property accessible, cleared, and — ideally — with someone there to receive the crew. If you can't be there, arrange a trusted contact with a key and a phone.</p>

      <h2>Plan the Night Before</h2>
      <ul>
        <li>Pack an overnight bag — you may arrive after shops close.</li>
        <li>Keep valuables, medicine and important documents with you, not the van.</li>
        <li>Charge phones and have the new address + directions to hand.</li>
        <li>Consider a fuel-friendly route in advance to avoid surprises.</li>
      </ul>
