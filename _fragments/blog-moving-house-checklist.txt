      <h2>8 Weeks Out</h2>
      <ul>
        <li>Book your <a href="../services/house-removals.html">removal team</a> early — popular dates go fast.</li>
        <li>Get three quotes, compare like-for-like, and check insurance cover.</li>
        <li>Start decluttering room by room. Sell, donate or bin — don't pay to move it.</li>
      </ul>

      <h2>6 Weeks Out</h2>
      <ul>
        <li>Book a skip or bulky waste collection for the big stuff you're not taking.</li>
        <li>Request valuation/redirection for any specialist items like pianos — see our <a href="piano-move-guide.html">piano guide</a>.</li>
        <li>Arrange schools, nurseries and dentist/GP transfer letters.</li>
        <li>Order packing materials (or book our <a href="../services/packing-services.html">packing service</a>).</li>
      </ul>

      <h2>4 Weeks Out</h2>
      <ul>
        <li>Book a moving-in date with your new utility suppliers (gas, electric, water, broadband).</li>
        <li>Notify the council for council tax at both properties.</li>
        <li>Start packing the rooms you use least — loft, garage, spare rooms.</li>
        <li>Update your address with banks, cards, insurers and subscriptions.</li>
      </ul>

      <h2>2 Weeks Out</h2>
      <ul>
        <li>Book the van, confirm the crew arrival time and finalise your inventory.</li>
        <li>Arrange parking permits or temporary road suspension if you need them.</li>
        <li>Take final meter readings at your old property.</li>
        <li>Pack your "essentials box" — kettle, mugs, toiletries, phone chargers, keys.</li>
      </ul>

      <h2>1 Week Out</h2>
      <ul>
        <li>Defrost the freezer and empty the fridge.</li>
        <li>Finish dismantling anything you're handling yourself (or book <a href="../services/furniture-dismantling.html">dismantling</a>).</li>
        <li>Separate what must NOT be packed — valuables, paperwork, medicine.</li>
        <li>Confirm keys/access arrangements with both agents or sellers.</li>
      </ul>

      <h2>Move Day</h2>
      <ul>
        <li>Label every box by room before the crew arrives.</li>
        <li>Be present to walk the team through heavy or fragile items.</li>
        <li>Keep the essentials box with you, in the car.</li>
        <li>Lock up, then check you have all keys before you leave.</li>
      </ul>

      <h2>After the Move</h2>
      <ul>
        <li>Read both meters and photograph the readings.</li>
        <li>Redirect your post with the Royal Mail redirect service.</li>
        <li>Register at your new GP and update vehicle/V5C documents.</li>
        <li>Change locks if you're moving into a previously-owned home.</li>
      </ul>
