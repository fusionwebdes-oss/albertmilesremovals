      <h2>Moving in Bilston: Access and Parking</h2>
    </div>
    <div class="prose">
      <p>Bilston has a varied housing stock. Streets close to the town centre are largely older terraces where access for a large vehicle can be tighter than on newer residential roads. Parking arrangements also vary, and some streets have permit zones or double yellow lines that affect where a van can stop.</p>
      <p>Tell us your address at the survey stage. We will check the approach at both ends of your move and plan around any access restrictions. If a parking suspension permit is needed on your street, we can arrange it ahead of moving day.</p>
      <p>Completion day delays are also more common than most buyers expect. If your keys are running late, see our guide on what happens when completion is delayed on moving day — it covers who to call, what the removal crew can do while you wait, and what happens if the van cannot unload that day.</p>

<section class="section">
  <div class="container">
    <div class="loc-section-head">
      <span class="kicker">Before You Book</span>
      <h2>What to Check When Booking a Bilston Removal Company</h2>
    </div>
    <ul class="loc-check">
      <li><div><strong>Insurance:</strong> Ask what goods-in-transit cover is included and at what value. All Albert Miles moves include cover up to £20,000 as standard.</div></li>
      <li><div><strong>Survey before quoting:</strong> A company quoting without seeing the property — in person or by video — cannot give an accurate price. Be cautious of phone-only quotes.</div></li>
      <li><div><strong>Written quote:</strong> Get the price in writing before you confirm. A verbal quote is hard to challenge if the figure changes on the day.</div></li>
      <li><div><strong>Availability:</strong> Book four to six weeks ahead where possible. Fridays and end-of-month dates fill fastest across the calendar year.</div></li>
      <li><div><strong>Delay policy:</strong> Ask what happens if your moving date changes or if keys are delayed on completion day. The answer should be in the contract.</div></li>
    </ul>
