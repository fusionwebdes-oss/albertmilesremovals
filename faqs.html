General Questions

Booking & Planning

How much do house removals cost?

House removal costs in the West Midlands typically fall between £350 and £1,200. The final price depends on the size of your property, how much furniture you have, the distance and the date. We're proud to offer affordable house removals — every quote is free, fixed and confirmed in writing, with no call-out fee and no obligation.

How do I get a removals quote?

Call us on 0121 679 2511 or complete the quick form on our quote page. We'll ask about your property, postcode and a rough idea of your belongings, then confirm a fixed removal company quote and our removals prices — usually the same day.

Can you do last minute or same day removals?

Yes — we regularly handle last minute removals, emergency removals and same day removals when availability allows, as well as busy weekend house removals. Call us on 0121 679 2511 and we'll do our best to fit you in.

Do you work weekends and evenings?

Yes. We're open Saturday 8am–4pm and can arrange evening moves for office relocations or tight completions. Weekend slots are popular, so book early.

Which areas do you cover?

We're a local removal company near me for thousands of households across the West Midlands — based in Birmingham and covering Wolverhampton, Walsall, Dudley, Solihull, Coventry and 20 more towns and cities. Whether you search for house movers near me or a removals company near me, we're the local team on your doorstep. We also handle long-distance moves anywhere in the UK.

How much notice do I need for a man and van?

Usually 2–7 days is plenty for a man-and-van job, but we've booked same-day moves when availability allows. Give us a call and we'll do our best.

On the Day

Insurance, Packing & Safety

Are you insured?

Yes. We carry up to £10m goods-in-transit insurance and public liability cover as standard. We also work to BAR (British Association of Removers) good practice guidelines.

Do you provide packing materials?

Yes. Boxes, tape, bubble wrap, tissue paper, wardrobe boxes and heavy-duty blankets are all available. See our packing services page for options.

Do you pack fragile items?

Yes. Our team is trained to wrap and pack glassware, china, electronics and artwork using the right materials and plenty of padding.

Will you dismantle and reassemble furniture?

Absolutely — beds, wardrobes, sofas and flat-pack items are dismantled safely, moved and rebuilt at your new home. See furniture dismantling.

Can you move a piano or large items?

Yes, we specialise in piano removals and awkward items. Tell us what you're moving and we'll bring the right equipment.

What should I do with my plants and fridge?

Defrost the fridge 24 hours before the move. Plants need a little water and careful positioning — we'll place them so they travel safely.

Do you offer storage if my dates change?

Yes. If there's a gap between leaving your old home and moving into the new one, our secure storage units can bridge it.
