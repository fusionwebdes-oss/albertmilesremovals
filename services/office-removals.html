Overview

Business Moves, Handled Properly

Every hour your business is closed during a move is revenue lost. Our office removal service is engineered around one goal: getting you open again as quickly as possible, with everything exactly where it needs to be.

We relocate offices, surgeries, clinics, salons, shops and warehouses across the West Midlands — and because we move businesses day in, day out, we understand the things that matter to you: IT and phone systems, sensitive documents, fragile equipment, staff desks and the sheer volume of a working office.

Most of our commercial moves happen over a weekend or an evening so you don't lose trading time. We can also arrange IT disconnection and reconnection, furniture relocation between departments and full or partial packing of desks and files.

Office Move FAQs

Questions About Office Relocations

Can you move our office over a weekend?

Yes — most of our commercial moves are completed over a weekend or an evening. Tell us your ideal window and we'll plan the whole move around it.

Do you handle computers and IT equipment?

Yes. We use anti-static wrapping for screens and servers, and we can coordinate with your IT provider to disconnect and reconnect systems at the new site.

Do you move confidential documents?

Absolutely. Documents travel in secure, sealed boxes with a numbered inventory, so nothing is misplaced or left behind.

Will our staff have to help with the move?

No — that's the point. Your team labels their desks and we handle the rest. If you'd like packing help for files and personal items, our packing service covers it.

How far ahead should we book an office move?

4–6 weeks is ideal for larger offices, but we've planned complete relocations in under a fortnight when needed. The earlier the survey, the smoother the move.
