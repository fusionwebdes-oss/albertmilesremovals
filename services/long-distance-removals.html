Overview

From the West Midlands to Anywhere in the UK

Whether you're relocating from Birmingham to Cornwall, from Wolverhampton to Scotland, or returning home to the Midlands after years away, our long-distance removal service gets your belongings there without a scratch — and with honest, fixed pricing.

We run regular routes across the UK, which means we can often combine your move with our return journeys and keep costs well below the big national chains. You'll deal with the same local team from start to finish, and we'll agree a delivery window that works around your plans.

Long-distance moves need more planning — vehicle sizing, loading strategy, route planning and, on the day, everything protected for a long journey. That's exactly what we're experienced at. Add our packing service and we'll arrive with everything boxed, wrapped and ready for the road.

Long Distance FAQs

Questions About UK-Wide Moves

How long does a long-distance move take?

Most UK moves are completed within 1–2 days. We'll confirm a delivery window when you book and keep you updated throughout the journey.

Can you pick up and deliver on different days?

Yes — with our storage service we can collect early and deliver later, which is often cheaper and more flexible.

Do you go to Scotland and Wales?

Yes, we move to every corner of mainland Britain including Wales and Scotland, and to the islands on request.

Are belongings insured for long journeys?

Yes — up to £10m goods-in-transit cover applies to every long-distance move, with the option to add extra cover for high-value items.

Can I track the van?

Our vans are GPS-tracked, and your move coordinator can tell you exactly where your belongings are at any point.
