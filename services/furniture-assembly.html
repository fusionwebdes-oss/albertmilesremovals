Overview

From Box to Built, Properly

That flat-pack wardrobe with 14 pages of instructions has defeated better people than us — which is exactly why we offer furniture assembly. Our team assembles flat-pack furniture every week, so what takes you a frustrating evening takes us under an hour, with the shelves level, the doors aligned and the fixings tight.

We build wardrobes, chests of drawers, beds, desks, shelving, garden furniture, office workstations and even fitted kitchen units. We bring our own tools, work carefully in your home and take packaging away with us. Need the furniture moved first? Add our furniture removal service and we'll assemble everything at your new home.

Furniture assembly is especially popular with new homeowners who've bought a house full of flat-pack, and with busy families who'd rather spend the weekend exploring their new area than wrestling with an Allen key.

Assembly FAQs

Questions About Furniture Assembly

Do I need to provide tools?

No. Our assemblers bring everything they need, including drills, levels and fixings.

Can you assemble IKEA and other flat-pack furniture?

Yes — IKEA, Argos, Wayfair, made.com and just about every major flat-pack brand. We build them all regularly.

What if a part is missing or damaged?

We'll flag it to you and build what we can. Missing parts are your retailer's responsibility, but we'll give you the exact part names and numbers to request.

Do you take away the packaging?

Yes — we remove and recycle all cardboard and packaging as part of the service.

Can you also fix furniture that's already built?

Often yes — wobbly wardrobes, sagging shelves and loose doors are quick jobs for our team. Give us a call and we'll advise.
