Overview

Small Moves, Done Properly

Not every move needs a three-man crew and a 40-foot truck. For bedsits, one-bedroom flats, single items or a sofa that won't fit in your hatchback, our man and van removals are the sweet spot: two professional movers, a modern, clean van and a fixed price agreed before we start.

Unlike some "man with a van" operators, we're a fully licensed, insured removal company. Our man-and-van crews wear uniforms, bring furniture blankets and trolleys, and treat every job — however small — with the same care as a full house move. Whether you need moving van hire with driver for a day or a one-way drop-off, we've got you covered.

We cover the whole West Midlands and further afield for single item removals and small moves, and can also help with tip runs, furniture collections and deliveries.

Man & Van FAQs

Questions About Man & Van

How is man and van priced?

We price each job as a fixed quote based on the items and the route — not by the hour. You know the price before we start, and it won't change.

What size van do you use?

Our man-and-van service uses modern 3.5-tonne Luton and long-wheelbase vans with tail lifts — enough for a 1–2 bed flat in one or two loads.

Can you help carry items upstairs?

Yes — that's the whole point. Our two-man crews carry items to any floor, using lifts or stairs, with equipment for awkward items.

Do you do long-distance man and van jobs?

Yes. If the job's bigger, check our long-distance removals; for single items across the UK we'll quote a fixed door-to-door price.

How far ahead should I book?

A few days is usually enough. For urgent same-day or next-day moves, call us and we'll see what we can do.
