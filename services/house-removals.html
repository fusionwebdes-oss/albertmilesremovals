Overview

Complete House Removal Service

Moving house is one of the biggest logistical jobs you'll ever take on — and one you shouldn't have to do alone. At Albert Miles Removals, our house removal service is designed to take the weight off your shoulders completely. One team, one fixed price and every detail looked after, from dismantling the bed to placing the kettle in your new kitchen.

We're the local removal company West Midlands families choose for professional house removals. We've moved every type of home: one-bedroom flats in Birmingham's Jewellery Quarter, period terraces in Wolverhampton, four-bedroom family homes in Solihull and everything in between. Whatever your property, we turn up with the right crew, the right van and the right equipment.

We're a home moving company that handles everything from small house removals and flat removals to apartment removals and full family relocations — with affordable house removals and clear, fixed pricing whatever the size. As a domestic removals specialist and furniture removal company, we also move individual items and full furniture loads across the West Midlands.

House Move FAQs

Questions About House Removals

How much notice do you need for a house move?

Ideally 2–4 weeks for larger homes, especially on weekends. We can often fit in smaller house moves with just a few days' notice — call us and we'll check availability.

Can you pack my whole house?

Yes. Our packing team can pack an entire 3-bed home in a single day using premium boxes and materials. You can also choose to pack just the kitchen, the fragile items or nothing at all.

Do you dismantle and reassemble furniture?

Always. Beds, wardrobes, sofas, dining tables and flat-pack items are taken apart safely, moved and rebuilt in your new home. See furniture dismantling.

What if I need storage between moving dates?

Not a problem. Our secure storage units are ideal for bridging gaps between completion dates, and we'll deliver your belongings when you're ready.

Are my belongings insured?

Yes — up to £10m goods-in-transit cover as standard, plus public liability. We also follow BAR good practice guidelines.

What should I do to prepare for moving day?

Defrost the fridge, empty wardrobes of loose items, set aside essentials (toiletries, chargers, a change of clothes) and make sure access is clear. We'll send a full prep checklist when you book.
