Overview

Walsall Moves With Local Know-How

Walsall offers some of the best-value family housing in the West Midlands, and it's a borough people rarely leave. When they do move, it's often within the town or between Aldridge, Bloxwich and Pelsall — moves our crews make every single week.

We cover the whole of the WS postcode area: the town centre (WS1), Bloxwich and Mossley (WS3), Pelsall (WS3), Aldridge and Streetly (WS9), Brownhills (WS8) and the villages around the A452. For businesses, we relocate offices and shops across Walsall's industrial and retail estates.

Being 25 minutes up the M6 from base means your quote reflects local pricing — not a "distance charge" from a national firm. And on moving day, our crews already know the one-way system around the Saddlers Centre, the parking on Birmingham Road and the school-run timing through Aldridge.
