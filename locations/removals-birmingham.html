Overview

Moving in Birmingham, the City We Call Home

Birmingham is the second-largest city in the UK — and moving here comes with its own set of challenges. Parking permits, resident zones, the Birmingham Clean Air Zone, narrow Victorian terraces, converted flats above shops and apartment blocks with loading bays instead of driveways. As a local company, we've solved all of them, many times.

We cover every Birmingham postcode from B1 to B76: the buzzing city centre and Jewellery Quarter, the student areas of Selly Oak and Edgbaston, the family suburbs of Harborne, Kings Heath and Hall Green, and the more leafy corners of Moseley and Sutton Coldfield.

Because we're based here, there's no long-distance surcharge on your quote, and we know which streets let you park legally, which buildings have service lifts and which completions happen on the busiest moving days of the year. That local knowledge saves you money, time and stress on moving day.

Birmingham FAQs

Moving in Birmingham? These Answers Help

Do I need a parking permit for my move in Birmingham?

If you're on a permit-only street, yes — or we can arrange a temporary suspension via Birmingham City Council. We'll advise before moving day, and our crews know the parking rules in every district.

Will your vans be affected by the Clean Air Zone?

No. Our fleet meets the required emission standards, so there are no CAZ charges or delays for your move — even in the city centre.

Can you move a flat in a city-centre apartment block?

Yes. We regularly move flats in converted warehouses and high-rise blocks, booking loading bays and service lifts where required.

Do you move students in Birmingham?

Absolutely — Selly Oak, Edgbaston and the city centre are some of our busiest student-move areas. See student moves for student-friendly pricing.

What's the average cost to move within Birmingham?

Local moves within Birmingham typically range from £300 for a flat to around £1,400 for a large family house, depending on volume and date. Get a free fixed quote to be sure.
